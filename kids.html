💪 Why Your Body Exercise

Your body is like a supercar — the more you use it, the stronger it gets! Kids who move for 60 minutes a day are healthier and have more energy.

Try fun activities like dancing, biking,— or playing tag with friends. It's not just good for your body, but also for your brain!
